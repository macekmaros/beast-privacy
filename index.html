Privacy policy for BEAST

Last updated: July 17, 2026

BEAST ("the app") is developed by Maroš Macek. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

BEAST does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All workout data you enter — training plans, exercises, sets, reps, and related notes — is stored locally on your device only.

We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

In-app purchases

BEAST offers optional in-app purchases ("tips") to support development. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We do not receive or store your payment information — this is handled directly by Google or Apple in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play privacy policy
  • Apple privacy policy

Third-party services

BEAST does not share any data with third parties, because no personal data is collected by the app itself.

Children's privacy

BEAST does not knowingly collect data from children, as it does not collect personal data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact
