Privacy policy for BEAST

Last updated: August 23, 2026

BEAST ("the app") is developed by altspaze. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Your workout data stays on your device

Everything you enter in BEAST — training plans, exercises, sets, reps, weights, and related notes — is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no cloud sync. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

Anonymous usage analytics

BEAST collects anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used and where it can be improved. This is the only data that leaves your device.

What is collected:

  • Which screens you open
  • A small number of specific actions — for example, creating a training plan, creating an exercise, entering values into a workout, or changing the app language
  • Basic technical context sent automatically with each event: app version, operating system and version, device model, and a randomly generated identifier stored on your device
  • When a support purchase completes: the product identifier and its displayed price

What is not collected:

  • Your name, email address, or any account details — the app has no accounts
  • The contents of your workouts: no exercise names, weights, reps, or notes are ever sent
  • Payment details
  • Precise location
  • Screen recordings or session replays
  • Any identifier used to track you across other apps or websites

The randomly generated identifier is created on your device and is not linked to you personally. It exists so that repeat use can be counted as one device rather than many. Your approximate country or region may be derived from your IP address by our analytics provider; the IP address itself is not stored alongside your events.

Turning analytics off

Analytics are on by default and you can turn them off at any time in the app, under Settings → Privacy. Once turned off, the app stops sending usage data immediately, and on subsequent launches the analytics component is not started at all. You can turn it back on again at any time from the same place.

Who processes this data

Analytics are processed by PostHog, acting as our data processor. Data is hosted in the European Union. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising or profiling. No other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used in the app.

  • PostHog privacy policy

In-app purchases

BEAST offers optional in-app purchases ("tips") to support development. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We do not receive or store your payment information — this is handled directly by Google or Apple in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play privacy policy
  • Apple privacy policy

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving how the app works. Because the analytics data is not linked to your identity, we generally cannot single out or retrieve the events belonging to a particular person. You can stop all further collection at any time using the setting described above. If you have a question or a request relating to your data, contact us using the address below and we will help where we are able to.

Data retention

Analytics events are retained by our analytics provider for a limited period in line with the plan we use, after which they are deleted or aggregated. Locally stored workout data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

BEAST is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The analytics described above are anonymous and are not used to build profiles of any user.
